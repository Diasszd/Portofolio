# Dark Editorial Portfolio Design System

## Aesthetic & Tone
- High-contrast, dark crimson editorial poster style.
- Bold typography, dramatic scale contrast, stark hairline borders, refined spacing.

## Palette
- `--bg-base`: #0B0B0C (Deep matte obsidian background)
- `--bg-surface`: #121214 (Elevated card & grid surface)
- `--bg-crimson`: #590D0D (Deep crimson gradient base)
- `--accent`: #E11D23 (Vibrant crimson-red accent)
- `--accent-glow`: rgba(225, 29, 35, 0.28)
- `--text-main`: #F5F5F7 (Crisp off-white primary text)
- `--text-muted`: #8E8E93 (Cool grey secondary text)
- `--border-subtle`: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)
- `--border-accent`: rgba(225, 29, 35, 0.45)

## Typography
- Display Condensed: 'Anton', sans-serif (700) for monumental wordmarks and headlines
- Body & UI: 'Plus Jakarta Sans', sans-serif (400, 500, 600, 700)
- Script: 'Caveat', cursive (500, 600) for organic hand-written accents
